Why this is hard

Google map example

1

Combine changing signals

Live speeds + historical traffic patterns; closures and incidents inform routing.

2

Predict across connected roads

A graph neural network passes learned messages between road segments to capture spreading congestion.

3

Compare routes by predicted time

Predict travel times for groups of road segments (“Supersegments”), then rank candidate routes.

The challenge: predict conditions when you get there. Our hospital decision also adds offload and queues at the expected arrival time.

Current repo: estimated travel + offload forecasts; active runs project simulated queues. Google Maps opens for navigation; Google Routes integration is not yet connected.

Published approach (2020): Google Maps: traffic & routing · DeepMind: graph neural networks

Our competitor · Patient Destination Software

Assigns hospitals, but cannot see the queue en route

Use

A coordinator assigns hospitals by location, urgency and services.

Gap 1

Status can be outdated. Misses ambulances already on the way.

Gap 2

Offload status is tracked by radio or phone, not in the tool.

Waypoint counts inbound ambulances to predict the queue at arrival.

Sources: Toronto Paramedic Association, Kelly interview.

Ambulance offload prediction

Our AI model predicts time to patient handover

A regression model learns how offload time varies by hospital, hour of arrival and day of the week.

Training data

36,774 records

Source dataset of 50,000 offloads across 11 Sacramento County hospitals.

Performance on 7,802 held-out cases

18.43 min

The model reduced average absolute error by 0.29 minutes per case (about 17 seconds). Toronto accuracy still needs local validation.
